# OpenDeviceIO names and marks

This policy covers the names **"OpenDeviceIO"** and **"ODIO"** and the project's logo.
It is separate from the licenses: the code and schemas are Apache-2.0, and the
specification, documentation, and registry data are CC BY 4.0. None of those licenses
grants rights in the names. Apache-2.0, for example, excludes trademarks in section 6.

## Who holds the names

The names are held by **Austin Howell, as maintainer, on behalf of the project**, until
OpenDeviceIO has a neutral home such as a foundation. At that point they will be
transferred there. The names are not registered trademarks today.

## What you can do without asking

- **Describe compatibility factually**: "imports ODIO files", "exports `.odio`",
  "supports OpenDeviceIO 0.1", "reads the OpenDeviceIO registry".
- **Name an integration descriptively**, as long as it is clear who makes it. For
  example, "Example Tool ODIO Importer, by Example Co." is fine.
- **Refer to the project** in articles, documentation, talks, and comparisons, and
  link to <https://opendeviceio.org>.
- **Say a file conforms** to a version of ODIO if it validates against that version's
  published JSON Schema. That is the only conformance test; there is no certification
  program.

## What needs permission

- **Implying endorsement, partnership, or certification**, such as "ODIO certified",
  "official OpenDeviceIO plugin", or "endorsed by OpenDeviceIO", unless the project
  has agreed to it in writing.
- **Using the names as, or as the leading part of, your own product or company name**
  in a way that suggests it comes from the project.
- **Calling a modified, incompatible format "OpenDeviceIO" or "ODIO".** Forks are
  welcome under the licenses but should use a different name.
- **Using the project logo** other than to link to the project.

## Manufacturer and product names in records

Registry records and examples name real manufacturers and products only to identify
devices. Those names and marks belong to their owners. Their appearance does not mean
the owner endorses OpenDeviceIO, or that OpenDeviceIO endorses them. See the
[registry data license](https://opendeviceio.org/data-license#images).
